FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. Kindred is open source and the models are open weights. You only need a computer that can run them.

What hardware do I need?

A small model like Llama 3.2 3B runs on most laptops. 7–8B models are happiest with 16 GB of RAM or a GPU with 8 GB of VRAM.

Where are my chats stored?

In your browser's local storage on your own machine. Clearing site data deletes them.

Can I create my own companion?

Yes. Add an entry to web/characters.js with a name, greeting and personality prompt.
